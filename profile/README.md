# Regional Hub for Big Data in Brazil

Welcome to the Regional Hub for Big Data in Brazil GitHub Organization.

This organization hosts open-source projects, tools, methodologies, training materials, and research products developed or supported by the Regional Hub for Big Data in Brazil and its partners.

The content shared in this organization aims to promote innovation, collaboration, capacity building, and the responsible use of data science, Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, geospatial information, and emerging technologies for the production and dissemination of official statistics.

> The Regional Hub for Big Data in Brazil contributes to advancing the use of Big Data and innovative data sources to improve official statistics and support evidence-based decision-making across Latin America and the Caribbean.

---

## Our Mission

The Regional Hub for Big Data in Brazil was established to foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and innovation among National Statistical Offices (NSOs), international organizations, academia, and the broader data community.

Our mission is to:

- Promote the use of Big Data and non-traditional data sources for official statistics.
- Encourage the adoption of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in statistical production.
- Strengthen technical capacities through training, workshops, and collaborative projects.
- Foster regional cooperation and exchange of best practices.
- Support the modernization and digital transformation of statistical systems.
- Advance reproducible, transparent, and open scientific practices.

---

## Areas of Work

Our activities include:

### 📊 Big Data for Official Statistics
Development and application of methodologies based on mobile phone data, satellite imagery, web scraping, social media, administrative records, and other innovative data sources.

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Research and implementation of AI techniques to improve statistical processes, data integration, classification, estimation, and quality assessment.

### 🌎 Geospatial Information
Development of geospatial methodologies, spatial analytics, and integration of geographic information into official statistics.

### 📚 Capacity Building
Training programs, webinars, workshops, technical cooperation initiatives, and knowledge transfer activities across the region.

### 🔬 Research and Innovation
Applied research projects focused on emerging technologies, digital transformation, and modern statistical production systems.
